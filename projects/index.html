GRC Ops Lab

Operational GRC practice & compliance automation

A learning environment built to practise realistic GRC workflows while exploring which repetitive tasks can safely be automated without automating analyst judgement.

The first live scenario reconciles a fictional HR population against a training export and surfaces structured findings for investigation. The second combines customer assurance with vulnerability management, requiring stored answers to be validated against current operational evidence before a response is prepared.

The broader learning roadmap covers areas including risk, control testing, third-party risk, access governance, incidents, privacy and AI governance.

Research, practical guidance & staff training

Applied workplace research and training translating privacy and information-security requirements into practical decisions for non-technical staff.
