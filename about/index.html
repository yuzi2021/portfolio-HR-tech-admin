I am building toward technical GRC work at the intersection of customer trust, continuous compliance, security automation and increasingly AI governance.

My current focus is understanding how controls operate in practice: what evidence supports them, how exceptions and remediation are handled, and where automation can reduce repetitive work without replacing judgement.

Why GRC

I am drawn to GRC because it sits between systems, people and evidence.

I enjoy taking a requirement, understanding how it should work operationally, identifying what would demonstrate that it actually happened, and translating the result clearly for the people who need to act on it.

What my previous background contributes

Before moving toward technical security and governance work, I spent more than a decade in education, professional communication and content-focused roles.

That background gave me extensive experience explaining complex ideas, adapting information for different audiences, writing clearly and working across cultures and professional contexts.

Those skills now show up most directly in security awareness and customer-facing GRC.
