GRC Change Monitor

Horizon scanning

GRC foundations need systematic study, but the surrounding regulatory, guidance and standards environment continues to change.

I am building GRC Change Monitor to make horizon scanning structured rather than relying on random browsing.

Try interactive demo ↗

Why I am building it

I want a repeatable way to monitor authoritative cybersecurity, privacy and AI-governance sources while keeping regulatory interpretation human.

The system automates discovery and organisation. I remain responsible for deciding what a publication actually means, whether it is relevant and what I need to revisit in my learning.

Authoritative sources first

Target sources include:

European Commission / EU AI Office

European Data Protection Board

Traficom / NCSC-FI

Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman

NIST

Appropriate official standards-update sources

How it works

  1. Authoritative sources
  2. Scheduled retrieval
  3. Change detection
  4. Deduplication
  5. Structured record
  6. Human review
  7. Learning / GRC impact note

Detect automatically.
Interpret deliberately.

Detected automatically

  • New publication
  • Source
  • Publication date
  • Changed / new status
  • Broad category
  • Duplicate status

Reviewed by me

  • What type of source is this?
  • Why might it matter?
  • Does it change my current understanding?
  • Which GRC area connects to it?
  • What requires further reading?
  • Is any action actually justified?

The monitor is not intended to provide legal advice or automatically determine regulatory obligations.
