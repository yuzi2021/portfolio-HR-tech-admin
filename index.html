HR Technology & Systems · Data Protection · GRC · Automation

I work where HR systems and data protection meet. A Computer Science student with more than ten years in education, I am moving into HR technology and systems work — building tools that keep people data consistent across platforms — while keeping the data-protection strength I built through applied privacy research, GRC practice and staff training.

The two belong together. HR systems hold the most sensitive data an organisation has, and it is in the lifecycle gaps — stale access, mismatched permissions, manual admin work — that both the operational cost and the privacy risk show up.
