HR ↔ LMS Reconciler

A browser-based tool that compares HR and LMS exports and flags records that may need investigation — missing accounts, leavers retaining access, permission mismatches and training-rule gaps — then generates a remediation report. Runs entirely client-side. The focus is operational: identify the issue, give the administrator enough to investigate it, and make a repeated manual check systematic. Built for the kind of problem a learning platform such as Docebo, and an engagement tool such as Peakon, create in day-to-day operations — informed by researching those platforms, not by using them.
