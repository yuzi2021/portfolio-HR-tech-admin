I created a 57-page staff guide translating data-protection requirements into practical guidance and scenario-based learning for early-childhood education staff. The guidebook was legally reviewed and became the foundation for workplace privacy/security-awareness training.

Practical scenario-based decisions

Security awareness in context

The training uses realistic situations rather than presenting privacy and information security only as abstract rules.

The goal is to help staff recognise the decision they need to make when they encounter a real digital-tool, personal-data or security situation.

  • Deciding whether a third-party app is appropriate
  • Recognising when personal information needs additional protection
  • Safer device and account practices
  • Handling information-sharing decisions

What this taught me about human security

Policies only help when people can understand how they apply in context.

This work taught me to preserve the underlying privacy/security requirement while changing the language, examples and level of detail for the audience who needs to act on it.
